# Business Insights Report

## Project: Global E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 1. Introduction

This report presents important business insights derived from the Global E-Commerce Sales dataset.

The analysis covers 2,000 orders from January 2023 to December 2025. It focuses on revenue performance, product categories, regional sales, monthly trends, customer segments, and profitability.

## 3. Business Observations and Recommendations

### Insight 1: Furniture Generates the Highest Revenue

**Observation:**

The Furniture category generated total revenue of 256,274.68, making it the highest-revenue product category. Technology ranked second with revenue of 139,518.22.

**Business Recommendation:**

Focus on popular furniture products, maintain sufficient inventory, and monitor profit margins.

### Insight 2: Europe Has the Highest Regional Revenue

**Observation:**

Europe generated revenue of 137,006.20, followed by North America with 133,876.38. North America recorded the highest number of orders, with 578.

**Business Recommendation:**

Study successful sales strategies in Europe and investigate opportunities to increase revenue in North America.

### Insight 3: Monthly Revenue Fluctuates

**Observation:**

June 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue at 18,068.09, while February 2025 recorded the lowest monthly revenue at 7,337.28.

This variation indicates differences in monthly sales performance and may affect inventory and marketing planning.

**Business Recommendation:**

Investigate the factors behind monthly fluctuations and plan inventory and marketing activities according to demand.

### Insight 4: Office Supplies Has the Most Loss-Making Records

**Observation:**

Office Supplies had 224 records with negative profit, with a combined profit of -827.99 across those records. The dataset contains 272 negative-profit records overall.

**Business Recommendation:**

Investigate product costs, discounts, shipping expenses, and pricing before making changes. Negative-profit records should not be removed simply because they show losses.

### Insight 5: Standing Desk Converter Is the Top Product by Revenue

**Observation:**

Standing Desk Converter generated revenue of 46,614.22, making it the highest-revenue product in the analysis.

**Business Recommendation:**

Review its sales performance and availability, and investigate whether related products could provide additional sales opportunities.

## 4. Customer Insights

### Insight 6: Consumer Customers Generate the Highest Revenue

**Observation:**

The Consumer segment generated revenue of 256,287.74, followed by Corporate at 146,050.39 and Home Office at 82,221.21.

**Business Recommendation:**

Maintain customer satisfaction in the Consumer segment while developing targeted offers for Corporate and Home Office customers.

### Insight 7: Consumer Orders Represent the Largest Customer Segment

**Observation:**

The Consumer segment recorded 1,006 orders, compared with 623 Corporate orders and 371 Home Office orders.

**Business Recommendation:**

Continue monitoring customer satisfaction and look for opportunities to increase order frequency in other segments.

Note: These insights describe customer segment performance. Repeat-customer purchasing behavior was not separately measured in this analysis.

## 5. Overall Conclusion

The analysis shows that Furniture is the leading category by revenue, Europe leads regional revenue, and the Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales.

Monthly revenue varies considerably, and negative-profit records require further investigation.

These findings can help guide inventory planning, marketing decisions, customer targeting, and profitability reviews.

Further investigation is needed to determine the causes of sales fluctuations and losses.
